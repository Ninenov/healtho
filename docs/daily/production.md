# HealthOS -- Product Definition

## What to Build

HealthOS is a secure, AI-powered digital health platform that creates a
lifelong, unified medical record for every individual. Instead of
keeping healthcare data fragmented across hospitals, clinics,
laboratories, and pharmacies, HealthOS provides a single
patient-controlled health timeline that can be securely accessed by
authorized healthcare providers.

The platform should: - Maintain a lifelong digital health record. -
Aggregate data from hospitals, clinics, labs, and pharmacies. - Provide
AI-powered medical summaries. - Support emergency access to critical
medical information. - Enable secure sharing with patient consent. -
Scale from private clinics to national healthcare systems.

## Features

### Core Platform

-   Universal Health ID (UHID)
-   Lifelong health timeline
-   Secure cloud medical records
-   Consent-based data sharing
-   Role-based access control
-   Audit logs

### AI Features

-   AI medical summary
-   Medical record extraction (OCR + AI)
-   Health trend analysis
-   Medication interaction alerts
-   Duplicate test detection
-   Disease risk prediction (future)

### Patient Features

-   Personal health dashboard
-   Medication history
-   Vaccination tracker
-   Appointment history
-   Health reminders
-   Family record management
-   Document vault

### Doctor Features

-   Complete patient timeline
-   AI-generated visit summary
-   Clinical notes
-   Prescription management
-   Treatment history
-   Follow-up scheduling

### Emergency Features

-   Emergency medical profile
-   QR/NFC/Smart card access
-   UID lookup
-   Critical health information
-   Offline emergency profile

### Healthcare Provider Features

-   Patient registration
-   Electronic Health Records (EHR)
-   Lab integration
-   Digital prescriptions
-   Pharmacy integration
-   Imaging support
-   Billing & insurance integration

### Security

-   End-to-end encryption
-   Multi-factor authentication
-   Patient consent management
-   Tamper-resistant records
-   Backup & disaster recovery
